Key Formulas

Bend Allowance (BA):
BA = π × (R + K×T) × A/180

Bend Deduction (BD):
BD = 2 × (R + T) × tan(A/2) - BA

K-Factor:
K = t/T
where t = distance from inside to neutral axis

Setback:
ISSB = R + T
OSSB = (R + T) × tan(A/2)

Rivet Designation System

The first dash number indicates diameter in 1/32" increments.

The second dash number indicates length in 1/16" increments.

Example: AN470-4-6 = Universal head, 1/8" diameter (4/32"), 3/8" long (6/16")
